01.

Interview notes

When
September 2026
Role
Design lead
For
Recruiter platform

Problem

It is difficult for users, especially interviewers and hiring managers, to take accurate notes and score candidates fairly during an interview.

Context

Structured interviews have been part of the Alva offering for several years and have been scientifically proven to be the most consistent predictor of work success. It is critical to improve the interviewing experience for users of Alva and ensure that they are able to set up interviews with candidates, prepare the questions to cover all the key assessment areas, and then evaluate all candidates equally and without biases.

Problem statements

Based on user interviews and scraping of sales and customer success discussions.

  1. As a recruiter attending multiple back-to-back interviews, it can be difficult to take notes, score candidates and remember what was said when I don’t have time right after each interview to process.
  2. As the TA running the hiring process, I have to chase hiring managers and interviewers to score candidates and complete the scorecards.
  3. As the TA running the hiring process, I can see that different interviewers score candidates differently, while also scoring different candidates in the same process in an inconsistent way. For us, consistency is key to a structured and scalable process.
  4. As an interviewer, it is hard for me to keep a natural interview discussion going while also taking notes on noteworthy and relevant points.

Agentic transcription to scorecard

At around the same time, we released a flow that ties into this one and helps with agentic scheduling of interviews. Using a third-party tool for this also ensured that we had the tool available for capturing transcriptions during the interviews.

Once we had the transcription, the key question was how we could use it to provide the most value.

Our solution was to use the Alva agent, educated in research and hiring science, to help evaluate interview transcripts and provide suggestions on the notes.

Key goals

  1. Reduce the manual note-taking burden for interviewers
  2. Provide consistent, criteria-based scoring suggestions across interviews
  3. Surface key moments and quotes from candidate responses
  4. Help calibrate evaluations and reduce bias through structured feedback

Agent vs human

But hiring is sensitive and should be a human-led process. So we worked on defining some clear boundaries for the agent in the process.

The agent

Captures themes, suggests comments and scores, with evidence provided for each suggestion. Must wait for human scoring before revealing the suggested scores.

The human

Decides whether the agent’s comments are accurate and complete, adds their own insights, and must score the candidate themselves before they can see a suggested score from the agent.

Solution directions

The UI was simple but had to be seamless.

  1. A separate tab in the scorecard where users could see the suggested content and apply it if they approved. Discarded
  2. A toggle above each content block where the user could swap between their own notes and the agent’s suggestion, and apply it if they chose. Discarded
  3. The agent’s suggestion is automatically filled into the text box, with the suggested score blurred out until the user makes their own selection. Links are easily available to view the evidence behind the scoring. Chosen

Chat agent flow

Any customer pasting a transcription into the chat should be guided into the scorecard flow, so that they must review the notes and score the candidate themselves before they can publish the scorecard.

Outcomes

A key customer has started using this feature

Very recent release, with metrics set up to capture further insights
